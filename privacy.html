# Candy Towers & Candy Towers Controller — Privacy Policy Last updated: 14 November 2026. Publisher: Bekhram Salimov, Ukraine. Privacy/support contact: bahramsalimov89@gmail.com. This policy covers the present Android TV game and Android controller without advertising, analytics or in-app purchases. We do not operate a player-account service or gameplay cloud backend. A planned feature is not an active data practice; this policy must be updated before an advertising or billing integration is released. ## On your devices and local network The game stores preferences such as language, graphics, volume and hero choice on the host device. The controller may store the nickname and temporary session token locally to reconnect. You choose a nickname; do not use sensitive information. Nicknames and player slots are visible to other people in the local session. The phone sends its nickname, temporary random session token, button commands and connection heartbeats to your TV/PC host. The host sends game state back to the phone. Local network addresses are necessarily used to establish this connection. Traffic uses local HTTP and is not encrypted: use a trusted private network. This information is not sent by the apps to a publisher server or public leaderboard. Host player records are held in memory, expire after approximately 60 seconds without a heartbeat when the server checks the session, and are cleared when the host server stops. The most recent command queue is bounded to 256 events; leaving removes that player's queued events. No permanent match-history database is maintained. Locally saved settings/token may remain until app/site storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. ## Camera and permissions Camera access is optional and used only when you choose to scan a QR code. Frames are decoded on the phone, not uploaded, saved to a gallery or used for identification. You can enter the local address manually instead. Revoke camera access in Android settings. The controller can vibrate for feedback; INTERNET permission permits local network communication. The apps do not request microphone, contacts or precise-location access. ## Stores and support Google Play processes downloads, purchase/account information and any store-operated services under Google's own terms. We do not receive your full payment-card information. If you email support, we receive the address, message and files you send through our email provider to answer your request. Please omit unnecessary personal information. Support correspondence is normally retained for up to 12 months after resolution, unless a legal obligation or an ongoing dispute requires longer; this is the publisher's retention commitment, to be implemented in support operations. We do not sell personal data or use app data for targeted advertising. No third-party ad, analytics or crash-report SDK is included in this release. If we later add one, we will update disclosures and obtain any required consent before the relevant processing. Device/OS/store diagnostic services outside the apps are governed by their providers. ## Choices, children and rights You can leave the session, clear app/site storage, uninstall, revoke camera access, or contact us about support records. There is no publisher account to delete. Parents should help children connect only to a trusted home network and avoid identifying nicknames. We do not ask children for a date of birth or create a marketing profile. Final store audience/rating is supplied through the store's review process; this policy does not assign an age rating. Where relevant data-protection law applies, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a copy of your personal data, and complain to the competent supervisory authority. Session data remaining solely on your own devices is controlled there; we cannot retrieve a cloud record that does not exist. Support correspondence is processed to respond to your request and manage legitimate support/security needs, with additional legal obligations where applicable. Email providers may process correspondence outside your country under their applicable safeguards. We will publish material changes with an updated date and provide further notice/consent where required.
